Fun with Matter

Chapter 1:

Four teenagers are walking home from school when they are encountered by an alien teleportation - it appears as a dome over the top of them - and they are entrenched in a war on an alien planet.

For a moment.

They manage to save a fleeing robot from destruction just as the dome recedes and everything returns to normal.

The robot is a shapeshifter from the planet Starr. He is an Astarr - a species at the top of the galactic food chain. The galactic police of the Milky Way. He has come to Earth to give it the missing element it needs to enter a galactic civilization.

The Vulkraines who were attacking Starr know he's on Earth, and are coming...

The shape-shifter named A1 (who normally assumes Robot form) gives the four teenagers a piece of his species' technology - the ability to transform matter into elements and form new matter - and relies on them to help him fight off the coming invasion.
