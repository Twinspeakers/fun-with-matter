# Hydrogen (H)

**Element Key:** `h`  **Handling Tier:** Stable (Tier 6)  **Commodity Reference:** **0.010 MU/g** (≈ **1.0 MU** per 100g)

## Overview
Hydrogen is Element #1 — the OG. It’s the lightest atom in existence, so light that it basically wants to leave the planet and go be dramatic in space. Out there, it’s everywhere: stars are basically hydrogen doing a nonstop glow-up. Down here on Earth, it hides in plain sight — locked inside water (H₂O), living things, acids, and a ton of useful chemicals. You don’t “find” hydrogen so much as you extract it.

As a fuel, hydrogen has serious main-character energy. When it reacts with oxygen, it can release a lot of energy — and at the point of use, the “smoke” can just be water. That makes it feel clean… but don’t get cocky. Hydrogen is famously flammable, it leaks easily, and it’s tricky to store. In your inventory, it’s the kind of element that demands proper containers, careful valves, and a strict no-sparks policy.

Also: hydrogen is a cryogenic diva. It boils at around −253°C and freezes near −259°C. That’s so cold it can turn “fuel” into “frost magic.” Future upgrades could let hydrogen power engines, boost burners, or run ultra-cold systems — if you can keep it contained.

## Extraction Pace
Pinned extraction (Gathering) produces **1g per tick** at the following baseline rates:

- **Regular:** 3m 30s per 1g
- **Quick:** 1m 10s per 1g

## Practical Uses
- **Processing:** Common ingredient in upgrades and recipes that reference `h` grams.
- **Economy:** Can be bottled into **Test Tubes** to clear cabinet space and sold to the commodity buyer (low-margin by design).

## Handling Notes
- Higher-tier elements may require better containment and may be rarer in salvage and story rewards.
