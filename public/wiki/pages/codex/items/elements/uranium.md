# Uranium (U)

**Element Key:** `u`  **Handling Tier:** Singular (Tier 1)  **Commodity Reference:** **0.200 MU/g** (≈ **20.0 MU** per 100g)

## Overview
A heavy, energetic element associated with high-end power systems and late-game tech.

## Extraction Pace
Pinned extraction (Gathering) is **not currently available** for this element.

- Obtain it through **scrap distilling**, **loot**, or **story rewards**.

> If the matching Distillery Cabinet container is full, extraction pauses until space is freed.

## Practical Uses
- **Processing:** Common ingredient in upgrades and recipes that reference `u` grams.
- **Economy:** Can be bottled into **Test Tubes** to clear cabinet space and sold to the commodity buyer (low-margin by design).

## Handling Notes
- Higher-tier elements may require better containment and may be rarer in salvage and story rewards.
