## In-world
A small, disposable Vulkraine unit. Fast, reckless, and rarely alone.

## Under the hood

### Loot pool
When a Minion is defeated, it rolls a number of loot slots, then fills each slot from the **common** table.

**Slot count distribution**
- 1 slot: ~65.0%
- 2 slots: ~25.0%
- 3 slots: ~10.0%
- Range: 1–3 slots
