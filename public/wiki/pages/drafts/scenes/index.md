# Draft Scenes

This area is for scene drafts you can **preview in the Story frame** while you’re developing and writing.

- Start here: [[drafts_scene_list|Draft Scene List]] (auto-list + one-click creation)

## How to preview

1. Open any draft scene
2. Click **Publish to Story (dev)**
3. You’ll jump straight into the Story page and see it rendered
