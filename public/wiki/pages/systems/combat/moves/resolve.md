# Resolve

Endure and survive with at least 1 HP next turn.

## Stats

| Type | Kind |
|---|---|
| Normal | Utility |

## Effect

- For the **next enemy hit**, if you would be reduced to **0 HP**, you instead stay on **1 HP**.
- Consumed after it saves you once.

## Notes

- It's basically a one-hit "nope" for lethal damage.
