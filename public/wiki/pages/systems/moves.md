# Moves

> **Path:** Systems / Moves

Moves are the actions used in **Monster Brawl** combat.

- **Damage moves** have a **Type**, **Hits**, an **Accuracy Mult** (how easy the move is to land), and a **Max-Hit Mult** (how hard it can hit). Some moves also add a small **Flat Max Hit** bonus.
- **Utility moves** deal no damage and instead apply buffs/debuffs.
