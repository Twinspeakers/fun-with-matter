## Create new draft page checklist

When you’re adding a new scene, just follow this and keep rolling:

- [ ] Click **New draft scene** (above)
- [ ] Give it a **title** (human-friendly)
- [ ] Accept or tweak the **id** (machine-friendly)
  - Tip: keep it `draft_scene_<something>`
- [ ] Write the scene like a script (dialogue, narration, whatever)
- [ ] Hit **Publish to Story (dev)** when you want to preview it in the Story window
- [ ] If you want to reset, hit **Clear draft (dev)**

> Everything you write here is saved in this browser (localStorage). It won’t touch your repo files unless you later copy/paste it into the real wiki.
