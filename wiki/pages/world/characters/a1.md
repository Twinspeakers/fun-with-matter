# A1

> **Path:** World / Characters / A1

## Overview

A1 is the **shapeshifter vendor** with “I’ve seen weirder” energy: smooth, calm, and just a little too confident for someone handing out alien-tech like it’s pocket change. Their role is mentor-adjacent, but not warm-and-fuzzy — more like a game-master who drops tools on the table and watches what you do with them. A1’s motivation is opaque (by design): they want you to grow, but also want to measure you. Their fear, if they have one, probably isn’t “danger” so much as **wasted potential**.
