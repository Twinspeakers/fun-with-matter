# Jackson

> **Path:** World / Characters / Jackson

## Overview

Jackson is the **anchor** of the crew: steady voice, steady hands, and a stubborn habit of stepping in front of danger like he can physically block bad outcomes. He plays the shield by default, but he’s not fearless — he just refuses to let the others be the ones who pay first. His motivation is simple and heavy: **everyone goes home in one piece**. His fear is the inverse: being the last one standing because he hesitated.
